In short

  • Your journal is encrypted on your device with a key derived from your password. We cannot read your positions, trades, money or notes.
  • We keep your email, your account's security records, the profile questions you choose to answer, your Friends activity, and counts of how the app is used.
  • We never sell your information, and we use no advertising or third-party tracking.
  • Marketing emails only if you opt in. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • You can see, export, correct and delete your information. Deleting your account deletes it.

Riskbook is operated by Rom Goldberg, an individual residing in Raanana, Israel (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of the personal information described here, which means we decide how and why it is used. This policy applies to the Riskbook website and app (the “Service”) and should be read with our Terms of Service.

  • Advertising identifiers, or data from advertising or analytics tools. We use none.
  • Precise location, contacts, photos or other data from your device.

5. Who we share it with

We never sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only:

  • With service providers who run parts of the Service for us and may use the information only for that purpose: Vercel (website hosting and servers), Neon (database) and Brevo (sending service emails). They are bound by data-processing terms.
  • With your friends, only what you choose to show them and the messages you send to them.
  • With market-data providers, only as any website would. To show prices and market information, the app requests data from providers such as Yahoo Finance, CoinGecko, Polymarket and others — some through our servers and some directly from your browser. A direct request lets that provider see your IP address and browser details, as with any website you visit. No account information is sent to them.
  • If the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or us.
  • If Riskbook is transferred — for example to a company we create, or as part of a sale, merger or a larger project — your information may move with it. The new owner must respect this policy, and we will tell you before it happens, so you can delete your account if you wish.

We may publish or share aggregated statistics that do not identify anyone (for example, “most users are aged 25–34”).

Access to identifiable information inside Riskbook is limited to the operator and, in future, authorised team members bound by confidentiality, and only as their work requires.

6. International transfers

We operate from Israel, and our service providers store and process information in the United States and the European Union. Israel is recognised by the European Commission as providing adequate protection. Where information moves to a country without such recognition, our providers use safeguards the law approves, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep it

  • While you have an account, we keep the information above so the Service works.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your account, journal, profile answers, usage counts, friendships, shared summary and messages at once. Copies in our providers' backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Inactive accounts: if an account is not used for 3 years, we may delete it, after warning you by email where we have an address for you.
  • Security records of IP addresses are kept from a few minutes to an hour.
  • Guest visits store nothing about you. A guest receives a cookie valid for 12 hours that lets the app load market data.
  • We may keep consent records, and information the law requires, for as long as needed to show we complied.

8. Security and encryption

Your journal is encrypted in your browser with a key derived from your password before it is sent to us, and it is stored only in that encrypted form. We do not have your password or the key, so we cannot read your journal — and we cannot recover it if you lose both your password and your recovery key. Password reset by email is not offered at present; if it is introduced, this policy will explain how it affects encryption before it applies to you.

Information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), sign-in is protected against repeated guessing, and access to our systems is restricted. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Cookies and storage on your device

We use only what the Service needs to work, so no cookie banner is required:

  • Sign-in cookie (pt_session): keeps you signed in for up to 30 days.
  • Guest cookie (pt_guest): lets a guest load market data, for 12 hours.
  • Storage in your browser: your preferences (such as layout and theme), data cached to make the app faster, and, while a tab is open, the key that unlocks your journal so you need not type your password again in that tab.

We use no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking cookies.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under Israel's Privacy Protection Law and, in Europe and the UK, the GDPR), you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it — you can delete your account yourself in Settings;
  • receive it in a portable form — you can export your journal yourself in Settings;
  • restrict or object to certain uses, including use based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time, for example to profile answers or marketing emails;
  • complain to a data-protection authority (see section 15).

To use a right we cannot handle in the app, contact us (section 15). We will reply within 30 days and may need to confirm your identity first. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

11. California and other US states

If you live in California or another US state with a consumer privacy law, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, to request its deletion and correction, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. The categories we collect are in section 2, their sources are you and your use of the Service, and their purposes are in section 4. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and have not done so in the last 12 months. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

12. Marketing emails

We send news and tips about Riskbook only if you opt in, by ticking the box at sign-up or later in the app. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in any marketing email or by contacting us. We will still send emails the Service needs, such as security notices or important changes to these documents.

13. Age

Riskbook is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

For material changes we will tell you in the Service (and by email where we have one for you) at least 30 days before they take effect, and ask you to accept the new version. Minor changes that do not reduce your rights take effect when published, with the date above updated.
