Coming from the old single-file version? Your trades are still there.

Storage belongs to the exact address a page was opened from, so a journal written by the old file is invisible here. Nothing is lost — it just has to be handed over. It takes a minute:

  1. Open your old portfolio_tracker_pro.html the way you always did, and check your positions are on screen.
  2. Press F12, then click the Console tab.
  3. Press the button below to copy the line, paste it into the console, and press Enter.
  4. It saves portfolio-backup.json to your Downloads and prints how many positions it found.
  5. Come back here, choose that file above, and check the green line matches.

      
The line only reads. It changes nothing, so the old page is left exactly as it was.
