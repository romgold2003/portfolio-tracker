🏦 Interactive Brokers — Read-only sync

This connects to IBKR's Client Portal Gateway running locally on your computer. The app can only READ your positions — it cannot place orders, move money, or interact with your account in any way.

Setup (one time only):
1. Download the Client Portal Gateway from IBKR:
interactivebrokers.com → Trading → IBKR APIs → Client Portal
2. Unzip and run: bin/run.sh (Mac/Linux) or bin/run.bat (Windows)
3. Open https://localhost:5000 in Chrome → log in with your IBKR credentials
4. Come back here and click Connect below
⚠ When IBKR asks for permissions — select Read Only. Never grant order placement to a journaling app.
